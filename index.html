What to expect when we work together.

Who is this for?

PE-owned and VC-backed B2B companies that already know which accounts they want to win, and have a sales team that wants those people warmed up before outreach.

What do you need from our team?

Your target account and contact list (a CRM export is fine), a little of your founder's time for content each month, and CRM access so engagement and meetings flow back to sales.

We are already running paid media. What changes?

We don't replace your existing campaigns. We add a contact-level layer: ads that only reach the named people sales is working, with results reported in the CRM your team already uses.

Do you guarantee that only our list sees the ads?

Audiences are built only from the contacts we match on LinkedIn and Meta. Match rates depend on platform and data quality, so we report exactly how many people from your list were reached.

How will we measure whether it works?

Engagement from named accounts, qualified meetings booked from the list, and pipeline influenced — tracked in your CRM rather than a separate ads dashboard.

What happens after 90 days?

We review the results together. If it's working, we agree on a plan to continue. Either way, you keep the audiences, creative and learnings.
